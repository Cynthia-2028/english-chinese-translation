# 中译英案例对读

## 说明

这里只做一件事：把同一句汉语的两种英文摆在一起，让人看清差别发生在骨架，不在词汇。
每条四行。劣译一律语法可通、拼写无误——那些交给校对；这里的错全是结构错：主语选错、句型选错、该删的没删、该还原成动词的还挂在名词上。
读法：先盖住佳译，自己改一遍劣译，再看"差别"那行有没有说中你改的那一处。说不中，说明你动的是词，不是骨架。

## 一、流水句与合句

- **原文**（01）：快点，否则你要迟到。
- **劣译**：Hurry up, otherwise you will be late.
- **佳译**：Make haste, or you will be behind time.
- **差别**：otherwise 是副词，不能连接两个独立句；祈使句＋or 才是英语"否则"的固定框架。
- **原文**（02）：夜深了，路也走完了。
- **劣译**：The night was deep. The road had also been walked to an end.
- **佳译**：The night is falling and the road is coming to an end.
- **差别**：两个命题等重，用一个 and 连接、共用进行体；"路走完了"不能拿 road 作 walk 的受事。
- **原文**（03）：即使这些条件都齐备了，也未必就能学成。
- **劣译**：Even if these conditions are all complete, also not necessarily can learn successfully.
- **佳译**：Even if you have got all these favorable conditions, you cannot be sure to succeed in learning.
- **差别**：让步从句用 even if，主句补出主语 you，谓语落在 cannot be sure to；汉语的"也"不译 also。
- **原文**（04）：一家如此，则一家无忧；一国如此，则一国太平；天下如此，则天下太平。
- **劣译**：If a family is like this, then the family has no worry, if a nation is like this, then the nation is peaceful, if the world is like this, then the world is peaceful.
- **佳译**：If a family follows this way, that family will be free from worry; if a nation follows this way, that nation will be at peace; and if the entire world follows this way, it will find itself in perfect peace.
- **差别**：三项排比用分号断，末项加 and 收口；前两项重复 family／nation 立骨架，末项换 it 收束。
- **原文**（05）：他去年冬天病了一场，病好以后就搬到乡下去住，一直住到现在。
- **劣译**：He was ill last winter, after he got well he moved to the countryside to live, and he has lived there until now.
- **佳译**：He was ill last winter; after he recovered he moved to the country, where he has lived ever since.
- **差别**：三个命题分两层——"病"与"搬"是先后，"住到现在"是搬的结果；分号断第一层，where 从句挂住第二层。
- **原文**（06）：我因为怕赶不上火车，急急忙忙地走了。
- **劣译**：Because I was afraid I could not catch the train, so I hurried away.
- **佳译**：I hurried so that I might not miss the train.
- **差别**："怕……"是目的不是原因，用 so that…might；主句主语 I 与从句主语一致，不必重复。
- **原文**（07）：他不可能懒惰，因为他有很大的进步。
- **劣译**：He cannot be idle. Because he has made remarkable progress.
- **佳译**：He cannot be idle, for he makes remarkable progress.
- **差别**：后句是追加理由而非直接原因，用 for 引导并列句；because 不能悬空成独立句。
- **原文**（08）：他半闭着眼睛，好像在回想什么。
- **劣译**：He half-closed his eyes, he seemed to be recalling something.
- **佳译**：He half-closed his eyes as though trying to recall something.
- **差别**：第二小句不是独立命题，改 as though＋分词挂在主句后；逗号粘连随之消失。

## 二、无主句与主语补出

- **原文**（09）：打捞沉船是不可能的。
- **劣译**：Raise the sunken ship is impossible.
- **佳译**：It is impossible to raise the sunken ship.
- **差别**：动词短语不能占主语位，须用 it 作形式主语、真主语（不定式）后置。
- **原文**（10）：坐船回家要五天。
- **劣译**：By boat go home needs five days.
- **佳译**：It takes me five days to return home by steamer.
- **差别**：it takes＋人＋时间＋to do 四段式；"坐船"降为 by steamer 作方式状语。
- **原文**（11）：谁都知道，在英国四五点钟是吃茶的时候。
- **劣译**：Who all know, in England four or five o'clock is eat tea time.
- **佳译**：As everyone knows, four or five o'clock is tea time in England.
- **差别**："谁"是虚指，作 as everyone knows 独立元话语；"吃茶的时候"落成 tea time，"时候"删。
- **原文**（12）：必须记住，压迫者与被压迫者被束缚在同一个社会里。
- **劣译**：Must remember that the oppressor and the oppressed are bound in the same society.
- **佳译**：It must be remembered that the oppressor and the oppressed are bound together in one society.
- **差别**：英语不留无主祈使式陈述；it must be remembered 保无人称，改主动则须补 we。
- **原文**（13）：听说他要来，我们都很高兴。
- **劣译**：Hear that he will come, we are all glad.
- **佳译**：The news that he will come gives us much pleasure.
- **差别**："听说"是传闻标记，改同位语从句挂 news；主语由"我们"换成 the news，谓语用 gives。
- **原文**（14）：昨夜发生了一场火灾。
- **劣译**：Last night happened a fire.
- **佳译**：A fire broke out last night.
- **差别**：汉语"发生"无主，英语取 fire 作主语、用 break out 自动词，时间状语后置。
- **原文**（15）：有生命就有希望。
- **劣译**：Have life have hope.
- **佳译**：While there is life, there is hope.
- **差别**：两个 there be 结构并列，while 表对照；汉语的"就"不译 then。
- **原文**（16）：据报道，今年粮食产量增长百分之五。
- **劣译**：According to report, this year grain output grew five percent.
- **佳译**：Grain output rose five percent this year, according to reports.
- **差别**：according to 短语后置，主语 grain output 提前；report 须加复数或冠词。

## 三、"把"字句与受事主语句

- **原文**（17）：这表要修。
- **劣译**：This watch needs to repair.
- **佳译**：This watch wants repairing.
- **差别**：受事主语＋want／need＋doing，主动形式表被动；用 to repair 会把逻辑主语错给手表。
- **原文**（18）：他抓住了我的手。
- **劣译**：He seized my hand and did not let me go.
- **佳译**：He seized me by the hand and would not let me go.
- **差别**：英语以人为宾语，部位用 the＋by 引出；物主代词 my 让位给冠词 the。
- **原文**（19）：他打了我的头。
- **劣译**：He struck my head.
- **佳译**：He struck me on the head.
- **差别**：同上一条，介词换 on；宾语位给人，部位作介词宾语。
- **原文**（20）：他们没有房子住。
- **劣译**：They have no houses to live.
- **佳译**：They have no houses to live in.
- **差别**：live 不及物，不定式作定语时须保留介词 in，否则悬空无着落。
- **原文**（21）：强盗抢了他的钱。
- **劣译**：The robber robbed his money.
- **佳译**：A highwayman robbed him of his money.
- **差别**：rob 的宾语是被抢的人，被抢物用 of 引出；直接接物则搭配不成立。
- **原文**（22）：供给我许多消息。
- **劣译**：furnish a lot of information to me.
- **佳译**：furnish me with a lot of information.
- **差别**：furnish 以受供者作宾语、物用 with；与 rob 的 of 框架正好相反，两类须分开记。
- **原文**（23）：他说话把声音都说哑了。
- **劣译**：He talked his voice hoarse.
- **佳译**：He talked himself hoarse.
- **差别**：结果补语前须用反身代词占宾语位（SVOC），"声音"不能当这个宾语。
- **原文**（24）：这个威胁他无法实行。
- **劣译**：He could not carry out this threat.
- **佳译**：This threat he was quite unable to carry out.
- **差别**：劣译语法无误，但把已知信息 this threat 推到句末，断了与上句的话题链；宾语前置才对得上上文。

## 四、范畴词与外壳名词

- **原文**（25）：计划工作中的严重错误
- **劣译**：a serious mistake in the work of planning
- **佳译**：a serious mistake in planning
- **差别**：the work of 是外壳，planning 本身已含"工作"；删后介词直接带动名词。
- **原文**（26）：促进和平统一事业
- **劣译**：promoting the cause of peaceful reunification
- **佳译**：promoting peaceful reunification
- **差别**：the cause of 是范畴词，宾语直接作 promoting 的宾语即可。
- **原文**（27）：保持密切合作的关系
- **劣译**：ensure a relationship of close cooperation
- **佳译**：ensure close cooperation
- **差别**：a relationship of 是空壳，"合作"本身就含关系义。
- **原文**（28）：这些困难是暂时性的。
- **劣译**：These hardships are temporary in nature.
- **佳译**：These hardships are temporary.
- **差别**：in nature 是"性"字的外壳，形容词已表性质，介词短语纯冗余。
- **原文**（29）：熊猫数量很少。
- **劣译**：Pandas are few in number.
- **佳译**：There are few pandas.
- **差别**：few in number 是硬译；改 there be 句型，让 few 直接限定名词。
- **原文**（30）：三个营的兵力被派去完成守卫仓库的任务。
- **劣译**：Three garrison divisions were sent to perform the task of guarding warehouses.
- **佳译**：Three garrison divisions were needed to guard warehouses.
- **差别**：perform the task of 是弱势动词＋名词，还原成 guard 一个动词。
- **原文**（31）：我们采取了退却的政策。
- **劣译**：We adopted the policy of withdrawal.
- **佳译**：We withdrew.
- **差别**：the policy of＋名词是汉语"政策"的直搬；一个动作就一个动词，主语仍是 we。
- **原文**（32）：中国人民是勤劳的民族。
- **劣译**：The Chinese people is a hard-working people.
- **佳译**：The Chinese are a hard-working and industrious people.
- **差别**：The Chinese 已含"中国人"，第二个 people 只作表语；主语是复数，谓语不能配 is。

## 五、四字格与成语的降级

- **原文**（33）：各人自扫门前雪，莫管他人瓦上霜。
- **劣译**：Everyone should sweep the snow before his own door and not care about the tiles of others.
- **佳译**：Just sweep away the snow in front of your own house, and never mind the frost on other people's roof, as the saying goes.
- **差别**：无对应谚语时直译意象＋as the saying goes 标记；后半句补出 frost，不做解释性改写。
- **原文**（34）：名师益友的指导启发
- **劣译**：the guidance and inspiration of famous teachers and good friends
- **佳译**：instructions and enlightenment from scholarly mentors and beneficial friends
- **差别**：四字格降为"形容词＋名词"；介词由 of 改 from（动作来源）；"名师"是 scholarly，不是 famous。
- **原文**（35）：丰功伟绩
- **劣译**：abundant merits and great deeds
- **佳译**：great achievements
- **差别**：两个近义四字并作一个"形容词＋名词"，删掉语义重叠的那一半。
- **原文**（36）：从容不迫
- **劣译**：calm and not hurried
- **佳译**：unhurried
- **差别**：对偶的后半纯为凑音节；英语只留一个词，否定式 not hurried 反而更弱。
- **原文**（37）：幸灾乐祸
- **劣译**：feel happy when others are in trouble
- **佳译**：take pleasure in others' misfortune
- **差别**：有现成习语就换，不铺陈"别人倒霉我高兴"的小句；小句一多，语气从讥讽变成说明。
- **原文**（38）：他的任务就是做一架桥梁或一条通路，以沟通作者和读者的心灵。
- **劣译**：His task is to be a bridge or a road, in order to communicate the hearts of the author and the reader.
- **佳译**：His task is to serve as a bridge or a passage between the author and the reader.
- **差别**："心灵"是范畴词，删；"沟通"不译 communicate，由 between 承担；"做"落成 serve as。
- **原文**（39）：鹤立鸡群
- **劣译**：like a crane standing in a flock of chickens
- **佳译**：stood out in a crowd
- **差别**：意象在英语里不可通，降为"动词＋介词短语"；直译反成异域装饰，读者要去猜喻义。
- **原文**（40）：千方百计
- **劣译**：by a thousand ways and a hundred plans
- **佳译**：by every means
- **差别**：数词是虚指，不落数；四字格整体换成一个限定短语。

## 六、逻辑显化（意合到形合）

- **原文**（41）：只要你一年内还我，我是可以把那笔钱借给你的。
- **劣译**：If only you return it to me within a year, I can lend you that money.
- **佳译**：I don't mind lending you the money provided you pay it back within a year.
- **差别**：条件用 provided (that) 比 if 更明确"以此为限"；"可以借"用 don't mind lending，比 can lend 更像答应。
- **原文**（42）：天虽则黑了，我们还是找到了回家的路。
- **劣译**：Although the sky was dark, but we still found the way home.
- **佳译**：Dark as it was, we found our way back.
- **差别**：although 与 but 不同现；让步用"补语＋as＋主谓"倒装，主句不再加 still。
- **原文**（43）：他到新加坡差不多五年了。
- **劣译**：He has been to Singapore for almost five years.
- **佳译**：It is almost five years since I came to Singapore.
- **差别**：持续时段用 It is…since＋起点；have been to 是"去过"，不含持续义。
- **原文**（44）：有人喜欢吃肥肉，另有人讨厌它。
- **劣译**：Some people like fat meat, other people hate it.
- **佳译**：Some people like fat meat, whereas others hate it.
- **差别**：两个独立句被逗号硬连（comma splice），须用 whereas 或分号；others 不能写成 other people。
- **原文**（45）：只要你看管好小钱，大钱就会看管自己。
- **劣译**：If you take care of the pence, the pounds will take care of themselves.
- **佳译**：Take care of the pence, and the pounds will take care of themselves.
- **差别**：祈使句＋and 等于 if 从句，比 if 更紧凑，也是谚语的定型骨架。
- **原文**（46）：惠子曰："我非子，固不知子矣……"（原文只说"曰"，未说"就着对方的话说"）
- **劣译**：Hui-tzu said, "Quite so. It is true that I don't know you…"
- **佳译**：Hui-tzu said, taking advantage of what the other had said, "Quite so. It is true that…"
- **差别**：分词短语把隐含的论辩动作显化，推理链清楚了，但补出的是原文没有的评论——显化到此为止。
- **原文**（47）：到了中世纪，这个节日改在二月十四日。
- **劣译**：In the Middle Ages the festival is supposed to have been fixed on February 14.
- **佳译**：In the Middle Ages the festival came to be held on February 14.
- **差别**：is supposed to 多一层"据说"，汉语无此义；显化不能替原文添传闻标记。
- **原文**（48）：英国之所以有民主政治，不是因为有了议会和内阁，而是因为有了富有民主精神的人民。
- **劣译**：It is not because of Parliament and the Cabinet that England has its democratic politics, but because of the people that there comes to be the English Parliament.
- **佳译**：England has its democratic politics not because of Parliament and the Cabinet but because of the people with their democratic spirit.
- **差别**：两个 that 从句平行，读者要等到第二个 that 才看清结构；改一个主句＋两个 because of，对举两侧同形。

## 七、动词名词化的双向调整

- **原文**（49）：求学是自己分内的事。
- **劣译**：To learn is one's own business.
- **佳译**：Learning is one's own business.
- **差别**：话题主语要用动名词，才能被后文 which 从句回指；不定式作主语太"一次性"，接不住下文。
- **原文**（50）：最重要的因素还是自己的努力。
- **劣译**：The most important factor is still one's own hard work.
- **佳译**：The most important factor consists in one's own effort.
- **差别**：consist in 只接抽象名词；"努力"是 effort，不是可数的 hard work。
- **原文**（51）：他不接受他们的提议是聪明的。
- **劣译**：His not accept their offer was wise.
- **佳译**：It was wise of him not to accept their offer.
- **差别**：评价句用 it＋of sb＋不定式；动名词复合结构作主语在此不合惯用法。
- **原文**（52）：他的伟大可以从他实行计划的毅力上看出来。
- **劣译**：His greatness can be seen from his perseverance of carrying out the plan.
- **佳译**：His greatness is seen in the perseverance with which he carried out his plan.
- **差别**：两个"的"字链改成一个介词＋关系从句；carry out 从动名词还原为从句谓语。
- **原文**（53）：我们必须改进我们的工作。
- **劣译**：We must make an improvement in our work.
- **佳译**：We must improve our work.
- **差别**：make an X in 是弱势动词＋名词，improve 一词承担全部；主语和宾语都不动。
- **原文**（54）：所有这些项目都应予以批准。
- **劣译**：Approval should be given to all these projects.
- **佳译**：All these projects should be approved.
- **差别**：被动＋名词化让施动者失踪；让受事作主语、动词作谓语，句子短一半。
- **原文**（55）：实行粮食配给。
- **劣译**：Grain rationing was implemented.
- **佳译**：Grain was rationed.
- **差别**：implement 是空壳动词，rationing 才是动作；直接让 grain 作被动主语。
- **原文**（56）：这一措施将限制投机者的活动。
- **劣译**：This measure will have a restrictive effect on the activities of speculators.
- **佳译**：This measure will restrict the activities of speculators.
- **差别**：have a … effect on 三词换一词；形容词 restrictive 还原为动词 restrict。

## 八、抽象与具体

- **原文**（57）：花在显露其本性时最美。
- **劣译**：Flowers are most beautiful when they show their nature.
- **佳译**：A flower is at its best when it has revealed its natural character.
- **差别**：at its best 是抽象概括，避开 most beautiful 的绝对化；单数 a flower 表类，不用复数。
- **原文**（58）：人也就达到了美的顶点。
- **劣译**：A man also reaches the top of beauty.
- **佳译**：A man may be said to have reached the zenith of his beauty.
- **差别**：zenith 与上句 at its best 分工，同义不重复；may be said to 把断言收弱一档。
- **原文**（59）：这就牵涉到文字学、论理学、文法、美学、心理学等知识。
- **劣译**：This involves the knowledge of philology, logic, grammar, aesthetics and psychology.
- **佳译**：This will involve such knowledge as philology, logic, grammar, aesthetics, and psychology.
- **差别**：such…as 直接引出列举，去掉"等知识"的 of 结构；knowledge 只出现一次。
- **原文**（60）：英国的政治是英国人民的精神造成的。
- **劣译**：English politics is caused by the spirit of the English people.
- **佳译**：It is the spirit of the English people that has made English politics.
- **差别**：cleft 把"精神"顶到焦点位；cause 太物理，make 在此是中性的"造成"。
- **原文**（61）：他的到来使大家很高兴。
- **劣译**：He came, and everyone was very happy.
- **佳译**：His arrival pleased everybody.
- **差别**：汉语两个小句降为一个名词主语＋情感动词；英语允许物称主语直接作施事。
- **原文**（62）：因为天气坏，我们未能动身。
- **劣译**：Because the weather was bad, so we could not start.
- **佳译**：Bad weather prevented us from starting.
- **差别**：原因从句升为名词主语 bad weather，谓语用 prevent sb from doing；because 与 so 一并消失。
- **原文**（63）：他来此何事？
- **劣译**：Why did he come here?
- **佳译**：What has brought you here?
- **差别**：汉语"人来"换成英语"事 bring 人"，物作主语；现在完成体表结果状态而非动作。
- **原文**（64）：海德公园正是民主精神充分发挥的地方。
- **劣译**：Hyde Park is the place where democratic spirit develops fully.
- **佳译**：Hyde Park is the very place where democratic spirit is fully demonstrated.
- **差别**："发挥"的主体是人，英语让 spirit 作被动主语；the very place 才译得出"正是"。

## 九、修辞降级

- **原文**（65）：你到那些地方去做什么？
- **劣译**：What do you go to those places for?
- **佳译**：Why should you go to those places?
- **差别**：should 表"何必"，保住反问里的不解与轻微责备；what…for 只剩问目的。
- **原文**（66）：安全第一，预防为主。
- **劣译**：Safety is first, prevention is the main thing.
- **佳译**：In industrial production, safety comes first.
- **差别**：两句口号并作一句判断；"预防为主"是第一句的同义补充，删。
- **原文**（67）：厉行节约，减少不必要的开支。
- **劣译**：Practise strict economy and reduce unnecessary expenditures.
- **佳译**：Reduce unnecessary expenditures.
- **差别**：前句是概括、后句是展开，同义；删概括句，只留可执行的那句。
- **原文**（68）：我们必须保持警惕，决不要放松戒备。
- **劣译**：We must maintain vigilance and never lose our guard.
- **佳译**：We must remain vigilant.
- **差别**：正反两说说的是同一件事，英语只留一面；强调靠词汇强度，不靠再说一遍。
- **原文**（69）：子固非鱼也，子之不知鱼之乐，全矣。
- **劣译**：You are not a fish, so your not knowing the happiness of fish is complete.
- **佳译**：You are not a fish; isn't it clear enough?
- **差别**：文言"矣"的断定语气靠反问承接；complete 直译成"完整"，读者不知所指。
- **原文**（70）：今夜的月光何等明亮！
- **劣译**：Tonight's moonlight is very bright.
- **佳译**：How bright the moon shines tonight!
- **差别**："何等"要求 how 型感叹，语序 how＋adj／adv＋S＋V；very bright 是陈述不是感叹。
- **原文**（71）：没有一个人老得不能学的。
- **劣译**：No one is old enough to not learn.
- **佳译**：No man is so old but that he may learn.
- **差别**：so…but that 等于 so…that…not，双重否定合成一个框架；not learn 的否定位置落错了。
- **原文**（72）：加强和改善宏观调控。
- **劣译**：Strengthen and improve macro-control.
- **佳译**：Strengthen macro-control.
- **差别**：and 两侧同义，"改善"未加新信息；判据是第二个词有没有带来区别。

## 十、语域与正式度

- **原文**（73，公文）：凡在本市行政区域内从事经营活动的单位和个人，均须遵守本规定。
- **劣译**：Everyone who does business in this city must obey this rule.
- **佳译**：All units and individuals engaged in business activities within the administrative area of this Municipality shall comply with these Provisions.
- **差别**：主语用 all units and individuals，定语用 engaged in 分词后置；"须"落 shall，"遵守"落 comply with。
- **原文**（74，公文）：本规定自发布之日起施行。
- **劣译**：This rule will start from the day it is published.
- **佳译**：These Provisions shall take effect as of the date of promulgation.
- **差别**：施行落 take effect，发布落 promulgation（法律动作名，名词化在此合法）；as of 引时间起点。
- **原文**（75，公文）：有下列情形之一的，不予受理。
- **劣译**：If you have the following situations, we will not accept.
- **佳译**：An application shall not be accepted under any of the following circumstances.
- **差别**：无主"的"字句转 shall 被动，补出受事 an application 作主语；"情形"落 circumstances，是术语不是范畴词。
- **原文**（76，通知）：兹定于本周五下午三时在会议室召开全体职工大会，请准时出席。
- **劣译**：It is decided to hold a meeting at three o'clock Friday afternoon in the meeting room, please attend on time.
- **佳译**：A general meeting of all staff will be held in the conference room at 3 p.m. this Friday. Please attend on time.
- **差别**：让 a general meeting 作主语、will be held 作谓语；地点在时间前，且与请托分成两句。
- **原文**（77，通知）：请各部门于本周五前将材料报送办公室。
- **劣译**：Each department please send materials to the office before Friday.
- **佳译**：All departments are requested to submit the materials to the General Office by Friday.
- **差别**：公文祈使不用 please，用 are requested to；"报送"落 submit，"前"落 by。
- **原文**（78，通知）：会议时间如有变更，另行通知。
- **劣译**：If the meeting time changes, we will tell you again.
- **佳译**：Any change in the time of the meeting will be notified separately.
- **差别**：条件从句压成名词短语 any change 作主语，谓语用 will be notified；全文不出现 we。
- **原文**（79，宣传）：我公司秉承"质量第一、用户至上"的宗旨。
- **劣译**：Our company holds the aim of quality first and customer highest.
- **佳译**：The company is committed to the principle of "quality first, customer foremost."
- **差别**：口号式并列降为引号内的固定短语；"宗旨"落 principle 不落 aim；主语用 the company 更正式。
- **原文**（80，宣传）：产品远销欧美各国，深受用户好评。
- **劣译**：Products are sold far to Europe and America, deeply loved by customers.
- **佳译**：The products sell well in Europe and America and are well received by users.
- **差别**：远销落 sell well in（主动表销路）；深受好评落 are well received，删"深"。
- **原文**（81，宣传）：欢迎垂询。
- **劣译**：Welcome to ask.
- **佳译**：For further information, please contact us.
- **差别**：汉语招揽语换成英语的 for further information 框架；welcome to 只用于邀请对方做某事。
- **原文**（82，学术）：本文认为，造成这一现象的原因主要在于制度设计上的缺陷。
- **劣译**：This paper thinks the reason of this phenomenon is mainly the defect of system design.
- **佳译**：This paper argues that the phenomenon is attributable primarily to flaws in institutional design.
- **差别**：argue 不用 think；"原因在于"落 be attributable to；"制度"用形容词 institutional。
- **原文**（83，学术）：上述结论仍需进一步检验。
- **劣译**：The above conclusion still needs to be further tested.
- **佳译**：These findings await further testing.
- **差别**：await 一个单词替代 need；still 与 further 语义重复，删一个。
- **原文**（84，学术）：数据表明，二者之间存在显著相关。
- **劣译**：The data shows there exists a significant correlation between the two.
- **佳译**：The data show a significant correlation between the two.
- **差别**：show 直接带宾语，删去 there exists 的存在句外壳；data 作复数，谓语用 show。

## 十一、学术与书面表达

- **原文**（85）：本文旨在考察流动人口的健康状况。
- **劣译**：This paper aims at the investigation of the health status of the floating population.
- **佳译**：This paper aims to examine the health of migrant workers.
- **差别**：aim to do 优于 aim at＋名词化；"流动人口"落 migrant workers，"状况"删。
- **原文**（86）：通过对 120 户农户的访谈，我们发现……
- **劣译**：Through interviewing 120 rural households, we found that…
- **佳译**：Interviews with 120 rural households reveal that…
- **差别**：through doing 让手段占了主位；改名词主语 interviews，谓语 reveal 把重心落在发现上。
- **原文**（87）：由于样本量有限，结论的推广需谨慎。
- **劣译**：Because the sample size is limited, so the conclusion should be careful to generalize.
- **佳译**：Given the limited sample, the findings should be generalized with caution.
- **差别**：given 是介词化的分词，省掉整个从句；"结论"不能"谨慎"，改被动、主语用 findings。
- **原文**（88）：这一发现与既有研究不一致，可能的原因有三。
- **劣译**：This finding is not consistent with the existing research, there are three possible reasons.
- **佳译**：This finding departs from existing research for three possible reasons.
- **差别**：逗号粘连；depart from 一句收拢，"有三"降为 for three reasons 作状语。
- **原文**（89）：本文将社会资本操作化为三个维度。
- **劣译**：This paper makes social capital into three dimensions.
- **佳译**：This study operationalizes social capital along three dimensions.
- **差别**："操作化"是术语 operationalize，不是 make into；along…dimensions 是固定搭配。
- **原文**（90）：表 1 报告了各变量的描述统计。
- **劣译**：Table 1 shows the description statistics of each variable.
- **佳译**：Table 1 reports descriptive statistics for all variables.
- **差别**：表格的动作用 report；"描述统计"是 descriptive statistics，取形容词形式。
- **原文**（91）：控制年龄与教育年限后，效应依然稳健。
- **劣译**：After controlling age and education years, the effect is still strong.
- **佳译**：The effect remains robust after controlling for age and years of schooling.
- **差别**：control for 才是"控制变量"；robust 是术语，"教育年限"落 years of schooling。
- **原文**（92）：该机制在东部地区尤为明显。
- **劣译**：This mechanism is especially obvious in the east area.
- **佳译**：The mechanism is particularly pronounced in the eastern region.
- **差别**：pronounced 是书面语；"东部地区"落 the eastern region，不再加 area。
- **原文**（93）：需要指出的是，本文的因果推断依赖于平行趋势假设。
- **劣译**：It should be pointed out that the causal inference of this paper depends on the parallel trend assumption.
- **佳译**：The causal inference here rests on the parallel-trends assumption.
- **差别**：It should be pointed out that 是元话语，整句删；rest on 替代 depend on；复合定语加连字符。
- **原文**（94）：本文的贡献在于提出了一个可检验的机制。
- **劣译**：The contribution of this paper lies in the proposal of a testable mechanism.
- **佳译**：This paper contributes a testable mechanism.
- **差别**：lie in the proposal of 是三重外壳；contribute 直接带宾语，主语从"贡献"换回"本文"。
- **原文**（95）：研究结果支持而非否定了这一假设。
- **劣译**：The results support this hypothesis and not negate it.
- **佳译**：The results support rather than refute the hypothesis.
- **差别**：rather than 连接两个同形动词，宾语只说一次；and not 不成对举格式。
- **原文**（96）：抽样采用分层随机方式，共获得有效问卷 986 份。
- **劣译**：The sampling used stratified random way, totally got 986 valid questionnaires.
- **佳译**：A stratified random sample yielded 986 valid questionnaires.
- **差别**：两个小句压成一句，"抽样"作主语、yielded 带结果；"共"不落 totally。

## 十二、汉语的"意"落到英文的"形"

- **原文**（97）：这场战争使我们的生意萧条。
- **劣译**：The war made our business not good.
- **佳译**：Our business has suffered not a little through the war.／The war has affected our business to a remarkable extent.／The war has rendered our business dull.
- **差别**：三句分别是 S+V、S+V+O+程度、S+V+O+C；首句以"生意"作主语便于承接下文，末句最书面。
- **原文**（98）：求学要靠自己。
- **劣译**：Learning must depend on oneself.
- **佳译**：Learning is one's own business.／One must learn on one's own.／Learning is something one has to do for oneself.
- **差别**：判断句适合段首立论；泛指主语 one 适合劝诫；第三句用关系从句，语气最软。
- **原文**（99）：这种态度我们不能接受。
- **劣译**：This attitude we cannot accept it.
- **佳译**：We cannot accept such an attitude.／Such an attitude is unacceptable to us.／It is such an attitude that we cannot accept.
- **差别**：宾语前置后不能再留代词 it；三句的焦点从"我们"移到"态度"，cleft 最重。
- **原文**（100）：他在会上没说什么。
- **劣译**：He at the meeting did not say what.
- **佳译**：He said little at the meeting.／Little did he say at the meeting.／He kept his silence at the meeting.
- **差别**：第二句否定词置首要倒装；第三句换视角，说"沉默"而不说"没说"。
- **原文**（101）：这座城市变化很大。
- **劣译**：This city changes very big.
- **佳译**：The city has changed a great deal.／Great changes have taken place in the city.／The city is not what it was.
- **差别**：第一句以 city 作主语；第二句以 changes 作主语，便于后文列举；第三句用否定比较，最口语。
- **原文**（102）：我们必须重视这个问题。
- **劣译**：We must pay attention to this problem.
- **佳译**：We must address this problem.／This problem demands our attention.／It is a problem that must be addressed.
- **差别**：第二句让 problem 作主语、demand 作谓语，语气最硬；第三句用 it…that 把问题推到焦点。
- **原文**（103）：他没有来，因为病了。
- **劣译**：He did not come. Because he was ill.
- **佳译**：He did not come because he was ill.／His absence was due to illness.／Illness kept him away.
- **差别**：第二句把"病"名词化作 due to 的宾语；第三句让 illness 作主语、keep 作谓语，重心从"他"移到"病"。
- **原文**（104）：这项政策效果不好。
- **劣译**：This policy's effect is not good.
- **佳译**：The policy has not worked well.／The policy has produced disappointing results.／The policy has failed to deliver.
- **差别**：第一句用 work 作谓语最轻；第二句名词化 results 便于后文评价；第三句 deliver 语气最重。

## 附：中式英文的十二个指纹

1. 一句五个逗号、主语换了三次——英语只认一个主干谓语，其余必须降级。
2. 句首直接是动词或形容词，全句找不到施事——主谓不可省，除非是命令句。
3. 见"进行、加以、予以、作出"就写 make、conduct、carry out——外壳动词后面挂着的才是真动作。
4. 见"情况、问题、工作、水平、层面"就照译 situation、problem、work、level——这些词多半是包装纸。
5. 四字格逐字译成两个近义形容词、用 and 焊住——第二个词往往不加信息。
6. 一段里三个 but、四个 so——连接词没有分级，逻辑反而糊了。
7. 每个小句都补上 because、if、although——显化成灾，替作者做了他没做的推理。
8. "在……下""通过……来""以……为"三连——介词框架套叠，动词被埋在最里面。
9. 句末落在 in some respects、to a certain extent、and so on——尾重丢了，读者记住的是废话。
10. 全篇谓语只有 is、has、make，具体动作都躲在名词里——动词虚弱。
11. 抽象名词作主语，谓语是 lie in、consist in、be reflected in——判断句套判断句。
12. 语法全对，可每一句都能逐词回译成汉语原句——骨架还是汉语的，只换了层皮。
